      Tips

      • Use ↑ ↓ and Enter to pick from the list, Backspace in an empty box to remove the last template, and Ctrl/⌘ + S to download.
      • The selection is stored in the URL, so you can bookmark it or share it, e.g. #macOS,VisualStudioCode,Python.
      • Templates such as Java, Gradle and AndroidStudio are kept in a fixed order so that later !pattern rules (like !gradle-wrapper.jar) still work.
      • +all variants of JetBrains IDEs ignore the whole .idea folder; +iml variants also ignore *.iml module files.
